Drink Water Reminder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Drink Water Reminder is a free app that reminds you to drink water. We collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no trackers.

What stays on your device

The time you type for your reminders and the number of glasses of water you log each day are saved only on your own device. We never receive this information. Clearing the app's data deletes it.

Notifications

The app asks for permission to show notifications so it can remind you to drink water. Reminders are created and shown by your phone itself. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not show advertising.

Children

The app is safe for children because it collects no personal information at all.

Health note

This app is a simple reminder and does not give medical advice. Ask a doctor how much water is right for you.
